Wwhoa... It's a.... It's a...!

You are a god-damn dracula!


"blehhhh bluhhh" - you

A horrendous and ghastly creature of the night, you have come to this station in order to SUCK as many people as possible and drink lots of BLOOD.

Please note: if you're sucking on people without drinking blood that's not allowed here at all and you will be asked to leave.

What you're here to do:

  1. Drink Blood
    • Use the Bite Victim ability button in the top left.
    • You may need to remove headgear first in case it's blocking neck access.
    • You must remain still while draining blood. If you move away from them, your blood-sucking bite will be broken!
  2. Escape (Probably!)
That's pretty much it!

Things to know:

  1. If you walk in space, starlight will burn you to a crisp. Which seems a bit silly because earth draculas aren't killed by starlight so, who knows.
  2. The chaplain is your nemesis. Avoid them and the chapel. However, they are weak to gun.
  3. New powers unlock when you collect a certain amount of blood (as indicated below.)
  4. Every time you use a power that isn't free, it depletes part of your blood reserve. Without enough blood, it will simply fail to cast.
